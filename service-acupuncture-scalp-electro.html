Acupuncture & TCM

Scalp & Electro
Acupuncture

Scalp acupuncture for neurological support and electro-acupuncture to enhance muscle stimulation and deepen therapeutic effect for chronic or complex conditions.

Book an Appointment Book an Appointment

What to Expect

  • Neurological intake and symptom mapping
  • Scalp zone needle placement
  • Electro-acupuncture attachment for targeted stimulation
  • Monitoring of sensation and response
  • Follow-up protocol planning

Who It's For

  • People with neurological conditions, post-stroke recovery, or chronic pain
  • Individuals with treatment-resistant presentations
  • Those seeking enhanced depth of acupuncture effect

Why Scalp & Electro Acupuncture

Scalp and electro acupuncture represent advanced specialisations within TCM. Dr. Viktoriia Taylor applies these techniques with clinical precision for complex cases that benefit from deeper neuromuscular engagement.
