Acupuncture & TCM

TCM Telemedicine
Consultation

Comprehensive Traditional Chinese Medicine diagnostics and consultation from the comfort of home. Ideal for follow-ups, herbal formula reviews, lifestyle guidance, and patients outside Calgary.

Book an Appointment Book an Appointment

What to Expect

A secure video consultation with Dr. Sitovska covering the elements of a TCM diagnostic that don't require hands-on assessment — your symptom picture, constitution, lifestyle, sleep, digestion, menstrual patterns, stress profile, and current concerns. You'll discuss your goals together and receive a clear, individualised plan for next steps.

  • Detailed symptom and lifestyle review
  • Visual tongue assessment via video
  • Discussion of constitution, patterns, and TCM diagnosis
  • Customised Chinese herbal formula recommendations (if indicated)
  • Lifestyle, dietary, and self-care guidance
  • Follow-up plan and treatment recommendations

Who It's For

  • Existing patients needing a follow-up or herbal review
  • Patients located outside Calgary or unable to attend in-person
  • Anyone exploring whether TCM is right for their situation
  • Patients managing busy schedules who prefer virtual care
  • Follow-up consultations for ongoing herbal formula adjustments

Two Session Options

Choose the length that fits the depth of conversation you need — a focused 30-minute session for follow-ups and herbal reviews, or a 45-minute session for new patients, complex presentations, or when starting a new herbal protocol.
