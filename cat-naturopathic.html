What Is Naturopathic Medicine?

Naturopathic medicine is evidence-based natural medicine that treats the root causes of chronic illness rather than masking symptoms. It draws on functional lab testing, nutrition, botanical medicine, and lifestyle strategies to address conditions conventional care often overlooks — from unexplained fatigue and hormonal imbalance to fertility and children's health.

As a naturopathic doctor in Calgary, Dr. Jill Nazar, ND, combines this whole-body approach with modern diagnostics for precise, personalized care.

Naturopathic Doctor in Calgary:
Our Approach

Every naturopathic visit at VeraVita begins with a comprehensive assessment — your health history, current symptoms, lifestyle, and goals. From there, Dr. Nazar builds a personalized treatment plan that may include functional lab testing, targeted supplementation, dietary strategies, and, where appropriate, IV nutrient therapy. This Expert, patient-centred process ensures your plan addresses the actual driver of your symptoms, not just the symptoms themselves.

Who Is a Candidate for
Naturopathic Medicine?

Naturopathic medicine is well suited for anyone dealing with chronic fatigue, hormonal imbalance, digestive concerns, unexplained weight changes, fertility challenges, or a child's ongoing health issue that hasn't been fully resolved through conventional care alone. It's also a strong fit for patients who want a deeper, root-cause investigation into their symptoms using functional lab testing.
