About Dr. Bennett

Dr. Sofia Bennett completed her undergraduate degree in Life Sciences at the University of Toronto before earning her Doctor of Naturopathic Medicine at the Canadian College of Naturopathic Medicine (CCNM). She went on to obtain advanced certification in bioidentical hormone therapy, giving her a deep and nuanced understanding of the intricate hormonal systems that govern so many aspects of women's health.

At VeraVita, Dr. Bennett works primarily with women navigating the challenges of hormonal imbalance, whether that presents as PCOS, PMS, perimenopause, or postpartum hormonal shifts. She also has a particular interest in the gut-brain-hormone connection, recognising that digestive health is often central to resolving hormonal and emotional wellbeing.

Dr. Bennett believes that truly effective medicine starts with listening. Her consultations are known for being thorough, empathetic, and empowering — helping women understand what is happening in their bodies and what they can do about it.

What Dr. Bennett Can Help With:

  • PCOS, irregular cycles, and hormonal acne
  • PMS and premenstrual dysphoric disorder (PMDD)
  • Perimenopause and menopause symptom management
  • Digestive disorders: IBS, bloating, constipation, food sensitivities
  • Fertility preparation and preconception care
  • Postpartum recovery and mood support
  • Anxiety, depression, and burnout related to hormonal imbalances

Her Approach:

  • Comprehensive hormonal and metabolic lab panels to identify imbalances
  • Bioidentical hormone therapy where appropriate, guided by labs and symptoms
  • Herbal medicine, clinical nutrition, and targeted supplementation
  • Gut microbiome testing and digestive restoration protocols
  • Mind-body strategies including stress resilience and sleep support
