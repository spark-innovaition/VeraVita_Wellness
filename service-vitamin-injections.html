What to Expect

  • Brief health intake and injection site assessment
  • Selection of appropriate injection type based on your needs
  • Fast 5–10 minute in-clinic administration
  • Minimal discomfort; resume normal activities immediately
  • Can be combined with IV therapy or naturopathic care

Who It's For

  • People with confirmed or suspected B12, D, or iron deficiency
  • Individuals wanting a quick energy and immune boost
  • Those seeking lipotropic injections for metabolic and weight loss support

Why VeraVita

Intramuscular injections are one of the fastest and most cost-effective ways to replenish critical nutrients. Unlike oral supplements, they bypass digestion entirely — making them ideal for people with absorption issues or those who simply want rapid results.
