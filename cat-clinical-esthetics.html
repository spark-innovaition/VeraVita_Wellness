Custom Relaxation Facial

A deeply restorative 60-minute facial tailored to your skin type — cleanse, exfoliate, mask, and moisture infusion for an instant glow.

European Facial + Lift Massage

A 90-minute European-style facial combined with lymphatic lift massage techniques for visible contouring, circulation, and radiance.

Anti-Aging Deluxe + LED

A comprehensive 120-minute anti-aging treatment combining advanced actives, multi-layer massage, and LED light therapy to visibly reduce fine lines and restore firmness.

Facial Deep Extraction

A targeted 60-minute extraction facial to clear congested pores, blackheads, and milia — leaving skin visibly cleaner, calmer, and more refined.

Acne Treatment + High Frequency

Targeted acne facial with high-frequency therapy to eliminate bacteria, reduce inflammation, and calm active breakouts — supporting long-term skin clarity.

Dermaplaning + Collagen Mask

Precise manual exfoliation to remove dead skin and vellus hair, followed by a restorative collagen mask — for instant brightness and smoother product absorption.

Sea-Nergy Green Peel

A 70-minute professional herbal peel using Sea-Nergy active botanicals to deeply resurface, brighten, and renew the skin — visibly smoothing texture and tone.

Microdermabrasion

Crystal or diamond-tip resurfacing to refine skin texture, fade hyperpigmentation, and restore a polished, even-toned complexion with no downtime.

Derma Needling

Advanced micro-needling to stimulate collagen remodelling, reduce scarring, improve skin texture, and restore a youthful appearance.

Exosome Derma Needling

Next-generation regenerative therapy combining micro-needling with exosome technology — deep cellular renewal, lifting, and skin restoration at a molecular level.

Exo-Hair Scalp Rejuvenation

Exosome micro-needling applied to the scalp to stimulate follicle activity, reduce hair thinning, and support healthier, fuller hair growth.

Skin Tag & Age Spot Removal

Precision removal of skin tags and age spot correction using targeted techniques — safe, effective, and minimal recovery time.
