About Dr. Rahman

Dr. Amina Rahman completed her medical degree at the University of British Columbia before specialising in aesthetic medicine through a fellowship with the American Academy of Aesthetic Medicine. She brings a rare combination of clinical precision and artistic sensibility to every treatment, believing that the best aesthetic results are those that enhance a person's natural beauty rather than alter it.

At VeraVita, Dr. Rahman consults with each patient to understand their aesthetic goals, concerns, and medical history before designing a personalised treatment plan. Whether addressing fine lines and wrinkles, volume loss, or uneven skin texture, her technique-forward approach ensures safe, natural-looking outcomes.

Dr. Rahman stays at the forefront of aesthetic medicine by attending international training programs and incorporating the latest evidence-based techniques into her practice.

What Dr. Rahman Can Help With:

  • Fine lines, wrinkles, and expression lines (Botox)
  • Volume loss in cheeks, lips, and under-eye hollows (fillers)
  • Skin texture irregularities and dullness
  • Acne scarring and enlarged pores
  • Sun damage and pigmentation concerns
  • Skin laxity and early jowling
  • Hair thinning (PRP scalp treatments)

Her Approach:

  • Detailed facial assessment and aesthetic goal-setting before any treatment
  • Conservative, natural-looking injection technique — "less is more" philosophy
  • Combination treatment plans for comprehensive rejuvenation
  • Full medical screening to ensure suitability and safety
  • Follow-up reviews at 2 and 4 weeks to optimise results
