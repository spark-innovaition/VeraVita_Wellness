Massage Therapy

Lymphatic
Drainage

Gentle, rhythmic massage to stimulate lymphatic flow, reduce oedema, support detoxification, and enhance immune function — available as full-body, facial, and Brazilian lymphatic drainage.

Book an Appointment Book an Appointment

What to Expect

  • Lymphatic health intake
  • Light rhythmic manual lymphatic drainage (MLD) technique
  • Specific focus on face, neck, abdomen, or full body as needed
  • Post-treatment hydration and movement guidance

Who It's For

  • People with oedema, post-surgical swelling, or lymphedema
  • Those seeking immune support or detoxification
  • Clients wanting facial depuffing or Brazilian lymphatic contouring

Why Lymphatic Drainage

Lymphatic drainage requires precise pressure and rhythm to be effective. Our RMT applies certified MLD technique to safely stimulate lymphatic vessels and support your body's natural drainage system.
