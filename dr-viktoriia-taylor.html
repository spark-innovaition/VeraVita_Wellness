About Dr. Taylor

Dr. Viktoriia Taylor brings a deeply holistic and deeply skilled approach to Traditional Chinese Medicine and acupuncture. Trained as a Doctor of Acupuncture, she draws on thousands of years of TCM theory and pairs it with advanced techniques including cosmetic acupuncture, Korean hand therapy, and scalp acupuncture — making her one of Calgary's most versatile acupuncture practitioners.

At VeraVita Clinic, Dr. Taylor works with patients experiencing a wide range of concerns — from chronic pain and stress to fertility support and cosmetic facial rejuvenation. Her initial consultations are thorough and unhurried, allowing her to understand each patient's constitution, health history, and goals before building a personalised treatment protocol.

Dr. Taylor has a particular passion for cosmetic acupuncture — a natural, needle-based approach to facial rejuvenation that improves skin tone, reduces fine lines, and promotes a healthy glow without injectables or downtime. She is also a sought-after paediatric acupuncturist, offering gentle, effective treatment for children aged 0–9 years.

What Dr. Taylor Can Help With:

  • Chronic pain — back, neck, shoulder, knee, and headaches/migraines
  • Stress, anxiety, and insomnia
  • Digestive disorders and IBS
  • Hormonal balance and fertility support
  • Cosmetic facial rejuvenation (natural alternative to injectables)
  • Paediatric conditions — allergies, digestive issues, sleep difficulties
  • Herbal medicine consultations and protocols

Her Approach:

  • 60-minute initial TCM consultation with tongue and pulse diagnosis
  • Personalised acupuncture protocols updated each visit based on progress
  • Integration of herbal medicine, cupping, and dietary recommendations
  • Cosmetic acupuncture series with measurable facial rejuvenation results
  • Gentle paediatric techniques adapted for children's comfort and sensitivity
  • Clear treatment timelines and realistic outcome expectations
