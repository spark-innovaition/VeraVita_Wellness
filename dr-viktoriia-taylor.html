About Dr. Taylor

Dr. Viktoriia Taylor is a Registered Doctor of Acupuncture, graduated from the Acupuncture and TCM College of Alberta (ACATCM) in Calgary and licensed with the College of Acupuncturists of Alberta. She brings to her practice over 10 years of knowledge and experience in the Western medical field as a medical doctor in Ukraine — giving her a uniquely integrative perspective that bridges Eastern and Western healing traditions.

At VeraVita, Dr. Taylor works with the full spectrum of TCM modalities, tailoring each treatment to the individual patient's constitution, symptoms, and health goals. She treats mind and body as a whole — a central principle of Traditional Chinese Medicine — and uses this holistic lens to address both acute and chronic conditions with precision and care.

What Dr. Taylor Can Help With:

  • Women's health, hormonal balance, and fertility support
  • Pain management — musculoskeletal, neurological, and joint conditions
  • Cosmetic facial acupuncture and natural rejuvenation
  • Digestive disorders and gut health
  • Trauma recovery and rehabilitation
  • Anxiety, depression, stress, and sleep disturbances
  • Chronic and complex health presentations

Her Approach:

  • Acupuncture treatments — initial consultations, follow ups, and combination sessions
  • Facial acupuncture for natural, non-injectable rejuvenation
  • Herbal treatment consultations and follow ups
  • Acupressure and seed therapy for children aged 0–9 years
