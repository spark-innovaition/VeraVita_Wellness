Your Aesthetic Physician

Meet Dr. Susan Mankaeeva, MD

Dr. Susan Mankaeeva is a Medical Doctor specialising in aesthetic medicine. Her approach combines clinical precision with a genuine artistic sensibility — ensuring every treatment enhances your natural features without looking overdone.

Whether it's softening dynamic wrinkles with neuromodulators or restoring facial volume with dermal fillers, Dr. Mankaeeva takes the time to understand your goals and deliver results that look subtle, balanced, and authentically you.

Treatments

What We Offer

Physician-administered aesthetic treatments that prioritise safety, precision, and natural-looking outcomes — every time.

Botox & Neuromodulators

Precision injections of Health Canada-approved neuromodulators to soften forehead lines, crow's feet, frown lines, and other dynamic wrinkles — naturally and safely.

Dermal Fillers

Hyaluronic acid fillers used to restore lost volume, define the lips and jawline, lift cheeks, and smooth deep folds — for a refreshed, youthful appearance.

Aesthetic Consultation

A thorough one-on-one assessment where Dr. Mankaeeva listens to your concerns, evaluates your facial anatomy, and recommends a personalised treatment plan.

Physician-Led Safety

All treatments are performed by a licensed Medical Doctor in a clinical setting — ensuring the highest standards of safety, hygiene, and therapeutic precision.

Touch-Up & Follow-Up

Post-treatment follow-up appointments to assess results and make any refinements — because your satisfaction is part of the treatment protocol.

Combination Planning

Strategic combination of Botox and fillers to achieve harmonious, full-face results — crafted to complement your natural features at every angle.
