Acupuncture & TCM

Traditional
Acupuncture

Classical Chinese needling to restore energetic balance, relieve pain, reduce stress, improve sleep, and support the body’s innate healing capacity — safe, effective, and deeply restorative.

Book an Appointment Book an Appointment

What to Expect

  • Full TCM intake and pulse/tongue diagnosis
  • Selection of acupuncture points based on your pattern
  • Sterile single-use needles inserted at specific meridian points
  • 20–30 minutes of restful needle retention
  • Post-treatment recommendations for diet, lifestyle, and follow-up

Who It’s For

  • People with chronic pain, stress, anxiety, or sleep issues
  • Patients seeking a natural, drug-free approach to healing
  • Those with digestive concerns, hormonal imbalances, or fatigue
