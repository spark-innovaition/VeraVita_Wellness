About Dr. Mitchell

Dr. Sarah Mitchell graduated with a Bachelor of Science in Kinesiology from the University of Alberta before completing her Doctor of Naturopathic Medicine degree at the Canadian College of Naturopathic Medicine (CCNM) in Toronto. Her background in human movement and physiology gives her a uniquely integrated perspective on how lifestyle, nutrition, and bodily function interconnect.

At VeraVita Clinic, Dr. Mitchell works with patients facing complex and often chronic health challenges — from fatigue and digestive dysfunction to autoimmune flares and hormonal imbalances. She uses comprehensive laboratory assessments, detailed health histories, and evidence-informed naturopathic therapies to uncover the root causes behind symptoms rather than simply managing them.

Her philosophy is grounded in the belief that the body has a remarkable capacity to heal when given the right support. She partners with each patient to build a realistic and sustainable plan that fits their lifestyle, values, and health goals.

What Dr. Mitchell Can Help With:

  • Chronic fatigue and low energy
  • Autoimmune conditions (Hashimoto's, lupus, rheumatoid arthritis)
  • Thyroid and adrenal dysfunction
  • Digestive disorders (IBS, SIBO, Crohn's, leaky gut)
  • Weight management and metabolic health
  • Preventive wellness and longevity planning
  • Blood sugar regulation and insulin resistance

Her Approach:

  • Thorough 90-minute initial consultations to understand your full health history
  • Advanced lab testing including nutrient panels, hormone profiles, and gut microbiome analysis
  • Evidence-based naturopathic therapies: botanical medicine, clinical nutrition, lifestyle counselling
  • Collaborative care — works alongside your existing healthcare team
  • Personalised follow-up plans with clear milestones and measurable goals
