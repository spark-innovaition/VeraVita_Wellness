Booking questions answered

Everything you need to know before your first visit.

Once you submit the booking form, our team will review your request and send a confirmation email or call within one business day. You will receive a reminder 24 hours before your scheduled visit.

No referral is needed. You can book directly with VeraVita Clinic. However, if you have existing lab results or reports from another healthcare provider, please bring them along — they are very helpful during your initial assessment.

Many extended health benefit plans in Alberta cover naturopathic medicine. We recommend confirming with your insurer prior to your appointment. We provide itemised receipts for all services so you can submit claims directly.

All aesthetic procedures at VeraVita Clinic are performed by trained medical professionals using approved Health Canada products. During your consultation, we review your medical history, discuss realistic expectations, and outline any possible side effects so you can make a fully informed decision.
