Acupuncture & TCM

Korean
Acupuncture

A refined needling system with a distinct meridian approach — highly effective for musculoskeletal pain, joint conditions, and neurological presentations alongside traditional protocols.

Book an Appointment Book an Appointment

What to Expect

  • Detailed assessment of musculoskeletal and neurological symptoms
  • Korean hand or body acupuncture point selection
  • Precise needling with a focus on distal and local points
  • Adjunct therapies such as moxibustion if appropriate
  • Personalised home care recommendations

Who It's For

  • Patients with joint pain, back pain, or sports injuries
  • Those with neurological conditions such as neuropathy or headaches
  • Individuals who have not responded fully to traditional acupuncture

Why Korean Hand Acupuncture

Our practitioner's dual Western and TCM training allows for a highly integrative assessment — choosing the most effective needling system for your specific condition.

Service Details

A specialised meridian-based needling approach for musculoskeletal and neurological conditions — integrating Korean and classical TCM systems for optimal results.
