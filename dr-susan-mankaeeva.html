About Dr. Mankaeeva

Dr. Susan Mankaeeva is a licensed Family Physician with a special focus on aesthetic and therapeutic treatments using neuromodulators and dermal fillers. She has been practising medicine in Canada since 2007 and is a proud member of CBAM, CPSA, and CCFP. With years of experience in injectables, Dr. Mankaeeva is dedicated to achieving natural, balanced results that enhance features and boost confidence.

Her expertise extends beyond aesthetics to skin health, acne management, and various dermatological conditions, offering professional medical advice grounded in years of clinical practice. She has completed advanced training in aesthetic medicine through courses across Canada and the United States, ensuring her clients receive the latest evidence-based care.

Outside of her practice, Dr. Mankaeeva enjoys exploring Alberta's stunning landscapes, travelling internationally, and spending time with her family.

What Dr. Mankaeeva Can Help With:

  • Dynamic wrinkles — forehead lines, crow's feet, frown lines (Botox)
  • Volume loss and facial contouring with dermal fillers
  • Lip definition and augmentation
  • Jawline sculpting and cheek lifting
  • Acne, acne scarring, and skin texture concerns
  • Skin health consultations and medical-grade skincare guidance

Her Approach:

  • A thorough aesthetic consultation before any treatment begins
  • Detailed facial analysis to understand your unique anatomy and goals
  • Conservative, natural results — enhancing your features without looking overdone
  • Only Health Canada-approved neuromodulators and hyaluronic acid fillers
  • Transparent pricing with no pressure — every treatment is your choice
