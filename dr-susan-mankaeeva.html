About Dr. Mankaeeva

Dr. Susan Mankaeeva brings the rigour of medicine to the art of aesthetic enhancement. As a fully licensed Medical Doctor with advanced training in facial injectables, she has spent years perfecting the balance between subtle refinement and natural beauty — a philosophy she calls "results that look effortlessly you."

At VeraVita Clinic, Dr. Mankaeeva offers physician-led Botox and dermal filler treatments in a calm, clinical environment. Every treatment begins with a thorough aesthetic consultation where she listens carefully to your goals, assesses your facial anatomy, and creates a personalised treatment plan tailored to your features — never a one-size-fits-all approach.

Her commitment to safety and precision is unwavering. As an MD, Dr. Mankaeeva has a deep understanding of facial anatomy, vascular safety, and injection technique — ensuring that every treatment is not only beautiful but performed to the highest medical standard.

What Dr. Mankaeeva Can Help With:

  • Softening fine lines and dynamic wrinkles with Botox
  • Restoring lost facial volume with hyaluronic acid fillers
  • Lip augmentation and definition
  • Cheek and jawline contouring
  • Preventing and reducing premature signs of ageing
  • Natural-looking facial balance and symmetry

Her Approach:

  • Complimentary aesthetic consultation — understanding your goals before any treatment
  • Physician-level knowledge of facial anatomy for safe, precise injections
  • Conservative, natural results — enhancing your features, not masking them
  • Transparent pricing — provided at consultation based on individual treatment needs
  • Follow-up care to ensure your results meet your expectations
