About Dr. Nazar

Dr. Jill Nazar is a licensed naturopathic doctor and a graduate of the Canadian College of Naturopathic Medicine (CCNM) in Toronto, ON. She is dedicated to helping her patients take an active role in their health journey through personalised, integrative care. With a focus on whole-body wellness, Dr. Nazar blends modern science with traditional healing methods to support her patients in achieving long-term balance and vitality.

Before becoming a naturopathic doctor, Dr. Nazar spent over a decade practising as a Medical Doctor in Central Asia, specialising in Pediatrics and Neonatology. Her work centred on general health, nutrition-related conditions, and weight management. After immigrating to Canada, she expanded on her medical expertise by transitioning into naturopathic medicine — bringing the best of both disciplines to her patients at VeraVita.

During her clinical internship at the Robert Schad Naturopathic Clinic, she also worked with diverse patient populations at the Flemingdon Community Health Centre, gaining valuable experience treating a wide range of health concerns across all ages. She is a proud member of the College of Naturopathic Doctors of Alberta (CNDA), the Canadian Association of Naturopathic Doctors (CAND), and the Alberta Association of Naturopathic Doctors (AAND).

What Dr. Nazar Can Help With:

  • Skin and dermatological conditions
  • Endocrine health and hormonal imbalances
  • Pediatric care — from infancy through adolescence
  • Diabetes management and blood sugar regulation
  • Digestive and gastrointestinal issues
  • Nutrition and sustainable weight support
  • Chronic fatigue and fibromyalgia

Her Approach:

  • Comprehensive initial consultations covering the full health history
