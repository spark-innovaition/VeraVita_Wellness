About Dr. Nazar

Dr. Jill Nazar founded VeraVita Clinic with a singular vision: to create a space where patients receive the depth of care they deserve. After completing her Doctor of Naturopathic Medicine at the Canadian College of Naturopathic Medicine (CCNM) in Toronto, she returned to Calgary to build a practice centred on thorough, compassionate, and evidence-driven naturopathic care.

Dr. Nazar has a particular passion for complex, chronic health concerns that often fall through the cracks of conventional medicine — fatigue, hormonal imbalances, fertility challenges, and metabolic dysfunction. She takes the time to understand the full picture of each patient's health history before building a treatment plan that is both realistic and effective.

As the clinic's IV Therapy lead, Dr. Nazar designs personalised infusion protocols using high-dose vitamins, minerals, and NAD⁺ — delivering nutrients directly where the body needs them most. She is also an experienced paediatric naturopath, bringing a gentle, child-centred approach to her youngest patients.

What Dr. Nazar Can Help With:

  • Chronic fatigue, burnout, and persistent low energy
  • Hormonal imbalances, PMS, and perimenopause
  • Fertility support and preconception care
  • Weight management and metabolic health
  • Paediatric naturopathic care (all ages)
  • Food sensitivities and digestive concerns
  • NAD⁺, B12, Vitamin D, and IV nutrient therapy

Her Approach:

  • In-depth initial consultations — understanding your full health history and lifestyle
  • Advanced functional lab testing including hormone panels, food sensitivity, and micronutrient analysis
  • Personalised IV therapy protocols tailored to your specific health goals
  • Botanical medicine, clinical nutrition, and lifestyle counselling
  • Collaborative care with your existing medical team where appropriate
  • Clear follow-up plans with measurable milestones and ongoing support
