What to Expect

The consultation is a brief, no-obligation phone call designed to help you understand whether osteopathy is the right fit for your needs. During this call, Amy will listen to your concerns, answer your questions, and explain how osteopathic care works.

  • Discussion of your current health concerns and goals
  • Overview of how osteopathic treatment can help
  • Answers to any questions or concerns you may have
  • Guidance on what to expect from your first appointment

Who It's For

  • Anyone curious about whether osteopathy is right for them
  • Those with musculoskeletal pain, headaches, joint restrictions, or chronic injuries
  • Individuals looking for a gentle, hands-on approach to healing
  • People wanting to understand the difference between osteopathy and other manual therapies

Why Start with a Consultation

A consultation gives you the opportunity to connect with your practitioner before committing to a full appointment. It ensures osteopathic care aligns with your needs and helps Amy understand your situation so she can provide the most effective care from your very first session.
