About Dr. Morales

Dr. Elena Morales earned her Doctor of Traditional Chinese Medicine (DTCM) and is a Registered Acupuncturist with the Canadian Acupuncture and Traditional Chinese Medicine Association. She also holds advanced training in fertility acupuncture and medical qigong, allowing her to offer a rich, multidimensional approach to healing.

Dr. Morales's philosophy bridges the ancient wisdom of Chinese medicine with current scientific understanding of the body's nervous and endocrine systems. Her acupuncture treatments are known for being precise, deeply relaxing, and highly effective — whether for acute pain, chronic tension, hormonal regulation, or emotional wellbeing.

At VeraVita, she works collaboratively with the naturopathic team to support patients through fertility journeys, post-injury recovery, anxiety management, and beyond. Her consultations are unhurried, intuitive, and tailored to each individual's constitution and needs.

What Dr. Morales Can Help With:

  • Musculoskeletal pain: back pain, neck tension, sciatica, joint pain
  • Headaches and migraines
  • Stress, anxiety, and insomnia
  • Fertility support and IVF preparation
  • Menstrual irregularities and hormonal balance
  • Digestive discomfort and nausea
  • Immune support and post-illness recovery

Her Approach:

  • Traditional Chinese medicine tongue and pulse diagnosis alongside modern intake
  • Individualised acupuncture point selection based on pattern differentiation
  • Integration of cupping, gua sha, moxibustion, and herbal prescriptions as needed
  • Nutritional and lifestyle guidance from a TCM perspective
  • Collaborative care with VeraVita naturopathic doctors for complex cases
