Massage Therapy

Massage Add-Ons

Enhance any massage session with therapeutic add-ons: aromatherapy essential oils, cupping therapy, or hot stone application — each designed to deepen your treatment results.

Book this service Book this service

What to Expect

  • Aromatherapy: Therapist-selected essential oil blends applied topically or diffused for mood, anxiety, or tension relief
  • Cupping: Myofascial decompression using silicone or glass cups to release deep tissue adhesions and improve circulation
  • Hot Stone: Integration of heated basalt stones into your massage for deeper heat penetration and extended relaxation
  • Each add-on is seamlessly incorporated into your existing session by your registered massage therapist

Who It's For

  • Any massage client looking to enhance their treatment outcome
  • Clients with stubborn deep tension, chronic stress, or circulation concerns
  • Those seeking a more immersive, results-driven treatment experience

Why Massage Add-Ons

Our add-ons are clinically effective tools — not just luxury upgrades. Each is thoughtfully selected to extend and deepen therapeutic outcomes, and can be combined with any standard, hot stone, lymphatic, or prenatal massage session.
