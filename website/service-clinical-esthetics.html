Your Esthetician

Meet Vialeta Bkharvani

Vialeta Bkharvani is VeraVita's dedicated Clinical Esthetician, bringing years of advanced skin-care expertise to every treatment. Her approach blends European skincare traditions with cutting-edge technology — including derma needling, exosome therapy, and professional-grade chemical peels — to deliver visible, lasting results.

Whether you're looking to address acne, fine lines, hyperpigmentation, or simply want a deeply restorative facial experience, Vialeta Bkharvani creates a fully personalised skin plan for every client.

Treatments

What We Offer

A full menu of clinical and relaxation treatments designed to address every skin concern — from your first facial to advanced regenerative therapy.

Custom Facials

From relaxation to European lift massage and anti-aging deluxe with LED — each facial is tailored to your skin type and concerns for an exceptional result every visit.

Derma Needling

Micro-needling therapy to stimulate collagen production, improve skin texture, reduce scarring, and restore a youthful, radiant complexion.

Exosome Therapy

Next-generation regenerative skin treatment using exosome technology — including Exo-Hair for scalp rejuvenation — for deep cellular renewal and visible lifting.

Dermaplaning & Peels

Dermaplaning with collagen mask, Sea-Nergy Green Peel, and microdermabrasion for a brighter, smoother, more even skin tone with no downtime.

Acne & Extraction

Targeted acne treatment with high-frequency therapy and deep extraction facials to clear congestion, reduce inflammation, and support long-term skin clarity.

Skin Corrections

Precision removal of skin tags and age spots, plus microdermabrasion treatments to resurface and refine skin texture for a clear, confident complexion.
