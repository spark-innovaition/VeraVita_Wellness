Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often — clear, honest, and helpful.

Group 1

Appointments

VeraVita Clinic offers a comprehensive range of naturopathic and aesthetic medicine services, including general naturopathic care, fatigue treatment, fertility support, weight loss programs, pediatric care, acupuncture and cupping, functional lab testing, IV therapy, vitamin injections, neuromodulators (Botox), and dermal fillers. Visit our Services page for full details.

You can book through our online booking form, call us at +1 (403) 000-0000, or email hello@veravitaclinic.com. Our team will confirm your appointment within one business day and send a reminder 24 hours before your visit.

VeraVita Clinic operates primarily by appointment to ensure every patient receives our full attention. We occasionally accommodate same-day appointments based on availability, so we encourage you to call us if you need to be seen urgently. Walk-ins cannot be guaranteed.

Your initial consultation is a thorough 60–90 minute session. Your naturopathic doctor will take a detailed health history, review your current symptoms, lifestyle, diet, and any previous test results. From there, a personalised treatment plan is developed. Please bring a list of current medications and supplements, any recent lab work, and your provincial health card.

Group 2

Insurance & Payments

No referral is required to see a naturopathic doctor at VeraVita Clinic. You can book directly with us. That said, sharing notes from your family physician or specialist can be valuable context for your practitioner.

Many extended health benefit plans in Alberta include coverage for naturopathic medicine visits. Coverage varies widely by plan, so we recommend contacting your benefits provider before your appointment. We provide detailed official receipts for every service to assist with reimbursement claims. Note that aesthetic services (Botox, dermal fillers) are typically not covered.

We accept Visa, Mastercard, debit, cash, and direct billing to select insurance providers. Payment is collected at the time of your appointment. For IV therapy and aesthetic treatments, a deposit may be required at the time of booking.

Group 3

Treatments & Safety

Patient safety is our top priority. All practitioners are fully licensed and registered with the appropriate regulatory bodies in Alberta. We follow strict sterilisation protocols, use single-use disposable equipment for all injections, and maintain a clean clinical environment that meets or exceeds all Alberta Health Services standards.

For non-urgent concerns after clinic hours, leave a message at +1 (403) 000-0000 or email hello@veravitaclinic.com and we will follow up the next business day. For urgent medical concerns, please call Health Link (811) or visit your nearest emergency room. If this is a life-threatening emergency, call 911 immediately.

VeraVita Clinic adheres strictly to Alberta's Health Information Act (HIA) and the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).
