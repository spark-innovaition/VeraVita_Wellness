FAQs

Questions? We're here to help

Everything you need to know before your first visit — clear, honest, and helpful.

All FAQs All FAQs
We offer a wide range of naturopathic and aesthetic services including naturopathic care, IV therapy, Botox, dermal fillers, acupuncture, cupping, fertility programs, weight management, osteopathy, massage therapy, lab testing, and vitamin injections.
You can book online via our booking page, call us directly, or send us an email. We typically respond within one business day and will confirm your appointment with all the details you need.
Most of our clinical services are in-person at our Calgary clinic. Select consultations and follow-up appointments may be available virtually — please contact us to confirm availability for your specific service.
Initial naturopathic consultations are typically 60–90 minutes, allowing your practitioner to conduct a thorough health history review. Aesthetic consultations are usually 30–45 minutes.
Many of our services may be covered by extended health benefits plans. We recommend checking with your insurance provider. Our team can provide receipts and any necessary documentation to support your claim.
