About Vialeta Bhharvani

Vialeta Bhharvani brings her positive energy and over 25 years of comprehensive nursing experience in dermatology and women's health to the VeraVita team. She has had a passion for esthetics and the beauty industry since an early age, and as a nurse she particularly enjoyed assisting doctors during various cosmetic procedures — seeing her clients' magical transformations firsthand.

After landing in Calgary in 2019, she successfully graduated from the Esthetic Institute Training Center in Calgary with an Advanced Clinical Esthetician diploma. She continuously applies her professional knowledge and practical nursing skills to carefully assess each client's skin needs and provide effective, innovative treatments that make every visit enjoyable and every result flawlessly beautiful.

Vialeta Bhharvani's background in clinical nursing gives her a uniquely informed approach to skin — she understands the physiology behind skin conditions and uses this knowledge to select and tailor the most effective treatments for each individual client.

What Vialeta Bhharvani Can Help With:

  • Dull, uneven, or congested skin in need of deep renewal
  • Fine lines and early signs of ageing
  • Acne, post-acne scarring, and hyperpigmentation
  • Dehydration, sensitivity, and compromised skin barrier
  • Anti-ageing and collagen-stimulating treatments
  • Brow and lash services for polished, defined features

Her Approach:

  • Thorough skin assessment before selecting any treatment protocol
  • Advanced clinical techniques rooted in her nursing and dermatology background
  • Derma needling to stimulate collagen production and skin renewal
  • Exosome therapy for cutting-edge cellular regeneration
  • Sea-Nergy Green Peel for deep resurfacing and skin revitalisation
  • A warm, personalised experience that makes every client feel truly cared for
