Massage Therapy

A full clinical massage program — therapeutic, lymphatic drainage, hot stone, prenatal, and paediatric care — delivered by a Registered Massage Therapist joining the VeraVita team.

Led by our Clinical Massage Specialist — Registered Massage Therapist (RMT)

Standard Therapeutic Massage

Deep tissue, Swedish, and therapeutic massage targeting muscle tension, chronic pain, and mobility restrictions — initial and consequent appointments available.

Lymphatic Drainage

Gentle, rhythmic massage to stimulate lymphatic flow, reduce oedema, support detoxification, and enhance immune function. Available as full-body, facial, and Brazilian lymphatic drainage.

TMJ Massage

Targeted intraoral and extraoral massage for temporomandibular joint dysfunction — relieves jaw tension, headaches, clicking, and restricted mouth opening caused by TMJ disorder.

Paediatric Massage

Gentle therapeutic massage for children aged 3–12, supporting growing bodies, reducing anxiety, improving sleep, and addressing musculoskeletal concerns in a safe, nurturing environment.

Hot Stone Massage

Warm basalt stones are used to melt deep muscle tension, improve circulation, and induce profound relaxation — a deeply therapeutic and restorative full-body experience.

Pre & Post Natal Massage

Specially adapted massage for pregnancy and postpartum recovery — relieves back pain, hip tension, and swelling during pregnancy, and supports healing and restoration after delivery.

Massage Add-Ons

Enhance any massage session with therapeutic add-ons: aromatherapy essential oils, cupping therapy, or hot stone application — each designed to deepen your treatment results.
