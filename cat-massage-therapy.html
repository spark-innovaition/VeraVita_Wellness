What Is Massage Therapy?

Massage therapy is the hands-on manipulation of muscles, connective tissue, tendons, and ligaments to relieve tension, reduce pain, and support recovery.

Registered Massage Therapists (RMTs) complete standardized clinical training and are regulated healthcare professionals, meaning treatment such as Therapeutic Massage is designed around your specific condition and functional goals, not just relaxation.

Massage Therapy in Calgary:
Our Approach

At VeraVita, every massage session begins with a discussion of your pain points, injury history, and goals — whether that's chronic tension relief, sports recovery, or prenatal comfort. Our Registered Massage Therapists then apply a personalized combination of techniques, from deep tissue work to gentle lymphatic strokes, to get you real, lasting results.

Who Should Get
Registered Massage Therapy?

Massage therapy is well suited for anyone managing muscle tension, chronic pain, sports injuries, postural issues, stress, fluid retention, or pregnancy-related discomfort. Because RMT treatment is regulated and clinical, it's also commonly covered under extended health benefits.
