About our Naturopathic
Care in Calgary

VeraVita Naturopathic Clinic is a Calgary-based integrative wellness clinic combining Naturopathic Medicine, IV Therapy, Acupuncture, Massage Therapy, and Osteopathy under one roof. Our Expert practitioners focus on root-cause, evidence-based care. Meet our Practitioners or book an appointment to experience patient-centred wellness care in Calgary.

At VeraVita, we believe healthcare is more than treatment, it's about building trust, fostering hope, and creating healthier lives.

From the very beginning, our mission has been to make evidence-based, patient-centred naturopathic care accessible to everyone, regardless of their background. Over the years, we've expanded our services, invested in modern treatments, and welcomed some of the best practitioners into our team. But even as we've grown, one thing has remained unchanged — our unwavering commitment to putting patients first.

Our Journey

Our journey through the years

2023
VeraVita Naturopathic Clinic Founded

Established in Calgary with a mission to bring holistic, patient-centred naturopathic care to the community.

Early 2024
IV Therapy & Injectables Launch

Expanded services to include IV nutrient therapy and vitamin injections, offering faster results for energy and immune support.

Mid 2024
Expanded Team & Fertility Program

Grew our practitioner team and launched a dedicated fertility program to support patients on their reproductive health journey.

2025
Acupuncture & TCM

Added Acupuncture & Traditional Chinese Medicine to our roster, bringing a complete integrative wellness offering under one roof.

2026
New Clinic & Expanded Team

Opening a new VeraVita location in Calgary, welcoming a Registered Massage Therapist and Osteopathic Manual Therapist to the team — bringing our full integrative vision to life.

Today
Continuing to Grow & Serve

We continue expanding our services and team to meet the growing needs of Calgary's health-conscious community.
