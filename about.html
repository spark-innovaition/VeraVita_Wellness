At VeraVita, we believe healthcare is more than treatment, it's about building trust, fostering hope, and creating healthier lives.

From the very beginning, our mission has been to make evidence-based, patient-centred naturopathic and aesthetic care accessible to everyone, regardless of their background. Over the years, we've expanded our services, invested in modern treatments, and welcomed some of the best practitioners into our team. But even as we've grown, one thing has remained unchanged — our unwavering commitment to putting patients first.

Our Journey

Our journey through the years

2014
VeraVita Clinic Founded

Established in Calgary with a mission to bring holistic, patient-centred naturopathic care to the community.

2016
IV Therapy & Injectables Launch

Expanded services to include IV nutrient therapy and vitamin injections, offering faster results for energy and immune support.

2018
Aesthetic Medicine Division

Launched aesthetic medicine services including neuromodulators and dermal fillers, completing our full-spectrum wellness offering.

2020
Expanded Team & Fertility Program

Grew our practitioner team and launched a dedicated fertility program to support patients on their reproductive health journey.

2023
5,000+ Patients Milestone

Proudly crossed 5,000 patients treated — a testament to our community's trust and our team's dedication.

Today
Continuing to Grow & Serve

We continue expanding our services and team to meet the growing needs of Calgary's health-conscious community.
