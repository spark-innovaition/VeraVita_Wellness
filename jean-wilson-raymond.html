About Jean-Wilson Raymond

I am passionate about creating a calm, relaxing experience for every client. In my free time, I enjoy staying active through biking, hiking, and daily exercise, along with stretching to support overall wellness. I find peace in simple routines like making tea and reading the Bible, and I love spending time outdoors gardening. I also enjoy handcrafting, which allows me to express creativity and mindfulness. My lifestyle inspires the care, balance, and attention I bring to each treatment.

Experience the art of refined massage therapy, where every detail is designed with intention and care. I offer a bespoke approach to bodywork, specialising in deep tissue, relaxation, hot stone therapy, cupping, lymphatic drainage, sports massage, and prenatal care.

Each session is thoughtfully curated to meet your body's unique needs, seamlessly blending advanced techniques with flowing movements and assisted stretching to enhance mobility, release tension, and restore balance.

In a calm, elevated setting, you are invited to fully unwind, recharge, and experience a level of care that is both therapeutic and indulgent — leaving you feeling renewed, restored, and truly revitalised.

What Jean-Wilson Raymond Can Help With:

  • Chronic muscle tension, knots, and deep-tissue pain
  • Stress, anxiety, and overall relaxation
  • Sports injuries and post-workout recovery
  • Limited mobility and range of motion
  • Lymphatic congestion and fluid retention
  • Prenatal discomfort and pregnancy-related tension

His Approach:

  • A bespoke, client-centred consultation before every session
  • Seamlessly blending deep tissue, relaxation, and sports techniques
  • Incorporating hot stone therapy and cupping for deeper relief
  • Assisted stretching to enhance mobility and restore balance
  • Lymphatic drainage to support circulation and detoxification
  • Creating a calm, elevated atmosphere for complete renewal
