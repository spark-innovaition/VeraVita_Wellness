Massage Therapy

Pre & Post Natal Massage

Specially adapted massage for pregnancy and postpartum recovery — relieves back pain, hip tension, and swelling during pregnancy, and supports healing and restoration after delivery.

Book this service Book this service

What to Expect

  • Pregnancy-safe positioning using supportive bolsters and pillows
  • Gentle massage targeting the lower back, hips, legs, and shoulders
  • Oedema reduction through lymphatic drainage techniques
  • Postpartum sessions focused on recovery, scar tissue, and hormonal support

Who It's For

  • Pregnant women at any trimester (with physician clearance)
  • Postpartum mothers seeking recovery and restoration
  • Women experiencing pregnancy-related back pain, swelling, or fatigue

Why VeraVita

Prenatal and postpartum massage requires specialised training to ensure both mother and baby's safety and comfort. Our registered massage therapist applies pregnancy-specific protocols to deliver genuine relief and support at every stage.
