Massage Therapy

Pre & Post Natal
Massage

Specially adapted massage for pregnancy and postpartum recovery — relieves back pain, hip tension, and swelling, and supports healing and restoration after delivery.

Book an Appointment Book an Appointment

What to Expect

  • Pregnancy-safe clinical positioning using supportive bolsters
  • Gentle massage targeting lower back, hips, and shoulders
  • Clinical oedema reduction through lymphatic drainage techniques
  • Postpartum sessions focused on recovery and scar tissue support
  • Guided stretches and restorative homecare recommendations

Who It's For

  • Pregnant women at any trimester seeking genuine physical relief
  • Postpartum mothers seeking structural recovery and restoration
  • Women experiencing pregnancy-related back pain, swelling, or fatigue

Why Pre & Post Natal Massage

Prenatal and postpartum massage requires specialised training to ensure both mother and baby's safety. Our RMT applies pregnancy-specific protocols to deliver effective relief while creating a nurturing environment for recovery.
