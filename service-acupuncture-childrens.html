Acupuncture & TCM

Children's Needleless
Acupuncture

Gentle, non-invasive acupuncture for children using non-needle techniques — laser, Shonishin tools, and acupressure — to support immunity, digestion, sleep, and overall wellbeing.

Book an Appointment Book an Appointment

What to Expect

Pediatric treatments are quick, gentle, and often entirely needle-free. Using non-invasive tools from the Japanese Shonishin tradition, we stimulate specific acupuncture points to regulate the child’s developing nervous and immune systems.

  • Detailed intake covering birth history, digestion, and sleep
  • Gentle tapping or stroking of meridians using traditional Shonishin tools
  • Cold laser or acupressure for targeted point stimulation
  • A calm, child-centered environment
  • Home care guidance for parents (massage or lifestyle)

Common Conditions Treated

  • Immune support (frequent colds, allergies, asthma)
  • Digestive wellness (colic, constipation, reflux)
  • Nervous system balance (anxiety, sleep issues, ADHD support)
  • Eczema and other inflammatory skin conditions

Service Details

Specialised pediatric care focused on safety, comfort, and natural developmental support.
