Massage Therapy

Standard Therapeutic
Massage

Deep tissue, Swedish, and therapeutic massage targeting muscle tension, chronic pain, and mobility restrictions — initial and follow-up appointments available.

Book an Appointment Book an Appointment

What to Expect

  • Health history intake focusing on pain, tension, and movement goals
  • Selection of appropriate techniques (deep tissue, Swedish, or combination)
  • Full-body or focused massage addressing primary areas of concern
  • Myofascial release and trigger point work as needed
  • Post-treatment self-care recommendations

Who It’s For

  • Clients with chronic muscle tension, back pain, or neck stiffness
  • Athletes or active individuals with recurring soreness or injury
  • Anyone seeking regular therapeutic maintenance for their body

Why VeraVita

Our Registered Massage Therapist brings clinical training and genuine therapeutic intent to every session — going beyond relaxation to address the structural and functional patterns behind your pain.
