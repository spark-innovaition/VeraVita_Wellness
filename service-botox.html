Neuromodulators &
Botox

\n

Precision neuromodulator injections to soften fine lines and dynamic wrinkles, restore a refreshed appearance, and prevent premature signs of ageing — with natural-looking results.

What to Expect

\n
    \n
  • Thorough facial assessment and injection mapping
    • \n
  • Medical consultation to discuss goals and suitability
    • \n
  • Conservative injections using Botox, Dysport, or Xeomin
    • \n
  • Results visible within 3–10 days; last 3–4 months
    • \n
  • Optional follow-up review at 2 weeks to optimise placement
    • \n
\n
\n\n
\n

Who It's For

\n
    \n
  • Adults looking to soften frown lines, forehead lines, or crow's feet
    • \n
  • Those seeking preventive Botox in their late 20s or 30s
    • \n
  • Patients wanting subtle, natural-looking facial refreshment
    • \n
\n
\n\n
\n

Why VeraVita

\n

Our injector combines medical precision with a refined aesthetic eye. We prioritise natural results — movement and expression are always preserved. Every treatment starts with a detailed consultation to ensure your goals are fully understood and safely achievable.
