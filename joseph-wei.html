A Holistic & Integrative Approach

Joseph is passionate about providing personalized, holistic care that addresses the root cause of health concerns while supporting long-term wellness, balance, and vitality. He provides treatments tailored to each patient's unique needs, combining acupuncture, TuiNa massage, cupping therapy, moxibustion, gua sha, electro-acupuncture, ear seeds, acupressure, and lifestyle recommendations to support optimal healing and long-term results.

How Joseph Can Help

He has extensive experience treating a wide range of acute and chronic conditions, with particular expertise in comprehensive pain management. Joseph frequently helps patients find relief from headaches, migraines, neck and shoulder pain, back and lower back pain, sciatica, knee pain, foot pain, sports injuries, workplace injuries, mobility concerns, and stress-related tension.

Joseph also has specialized expertise in weight management and detoxification, supporting healthy weight loss, metabolic balance, digestive wellness, and overall internal health. His clinical interests extend to women's health, fertility, hormonal balance, menstrual concerns, menopause and perimenopause support, as well as a variety of gynecological conditions.

In addition, Joseph has a special focus on vision and eye health for both children and adults. He works with patients experiencing conditions such as blurred vision, reduced visual acuity, myopia (nearsightedness), presbyopia, eye strain, and other vision-related concerns commonly associated with modern lifestyles.

Philosophy of Care

Committed to creating a welcoming and supportive environment, Joseph works closely with patients from diverse backgrounds to develop individualized treatment plans that promote lasting health and well-being.
