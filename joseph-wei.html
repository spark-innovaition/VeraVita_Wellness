About Joseph Wei

I am passionate about providing personalized, holistic care that addresses the root cause of health concerns while supporting long-term wellness, balance, and vitality. With over 20 years of clinical experience, I integrate the deep wisdom of Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) with modern diagnostic approaches.

I have extensive experience treating a wide range of acute and chronic conditions, with particular expertise in comprehensive pain management. I frequently help patients find relief from headaches, migraines, neck and shoulder pain, back pain, sciatica, sports injuries, and stress-related tension.

My clinical interests extend deeply into women's health—supporting fertility, hormonal balance, and menopause—as well as specialized expertise in weight management, detoxification, and pediatric and adult vision health (myopia, eye strain, and blurred vision).

What Joseph Can Help With:

  • Acute & chronic pain (headaches, migraines, back pain, sciatica)
  • Women's health, hormonal balance, fertility, and menopause
  • Metabolic balance, detoxification, and weight management
  • Vision & eye health for both adults and children
  • Stress, anxiety, insomnia, and nervous system regulation
  • Sports and workplace injuries

His Approach:

  • Integrating Traditional Chinese Medicine with modern diagnostic approaches
  • Customizing treatments using acupuncture, TuiNa massage, and cupping
  • Incorporating moxibustion, gua sha, electro-acupuncture, and ear seeds
  • Providing targeted lifestyle and herbal recommendations for long-term healing
  • Creating a welcoming, supportive environment for patients from diverse backgrounds
