About Dr. Torres

Dr. Michael Torres holds a Bachelor of Science in Biochemistry from McGill University and a Doctor of Naturopathic Medicine from the Boucher Institute of Naturopathic Medicine (BINM). His extensive background in cellular biochemistry gives him a sophisticated understanding of how nutrients interact at the molecular level — the foundation of his highly targeted IV therapy protocols.

At VeraVita, Dr. Torres designs and oversees all IV nutrient therapy programs. Each protocol is customised based on comprehensive blood work and a detailed health intake, ensuring that the right vitamins, minerals, amino acids, and antioxidants are delivered in optimal concentrations directly into the bloodstream for maximum absorption and effect.

Whether you're recovering from illness, preparing for a major athletic event, or simply seeking a foundational wellness boost, Dr. Torres's protocols are precision-engineered to meet your goals safely and effectively.

What Dr. Torres Can Help With:

  • Chronic fatigue, burnout, and post-viral exhaustion (Long COVID)
  • Immune dysfunction and frequent illness
  • Athletic performance and recovery optimisation
  • Nutrient deficiencies (B12, magnesium, vitamin C, zinc)
  • Hangover and acute recovery
  • Detoxification and heavy metal support
  • Skin health and anti-ageing nutrient support

His Approach:

  • Comprehensive micronutrient and blood testing before any IV protocol
  • Custom-formulated IV drips based on individual lab results and health goals
  • Safe clinical setting with continuous monitoring during all infusions
  • Integrated approach — IV therapy combined with oral supplementation and diet
  • Regular reassessment to track progress and adjust protocols
