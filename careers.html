Careers at VeraVita

Build your career where it matters

We're a team of dedicated practitioners united by one purpose — delivering exceptional, personalised care to every patient. If you share that drive, we'd love to hear from you.

Open Positions

We're currently hiring

Explore our open roles below. All positions are based at our Calgary clinic and offer a collaborative, patient-first environment.

Registered Massage Therapist (RMT)

Full-time Calgary, AB

We're looking for a compassionate, registered massage therapist to join our growing wellness team. You'll work alongside naturopathic doctors and other specialists to deliver integrated, whole-body care.

  • Valid RMT registration with CRMTA or equivalent
  • Experience in therapeutic and relaxation massage
  • Strong communication and patient-centred approach
  • Interest in integrative health and collaboration

Naturopathic Doctor (ND)

Full-time Calgary, AB

Join our naturopathic team and help patients address the root cause of their health concerns — from chronic disease and hormonal imbalances to digestive health and fatigue.

  • Licensed ND in good standing with CNDA
  • Experience in clinical naturopathic practice
  • Proficiency in functional and lab testing
  • Commitment to evidence-informed, holistic care
Apply Now

Acupuncturist

Full-time Calgary, AB

We're seeking a skilled acupuncturist to expand our traditional Chinese medicine offerings — supporting patients with pain management, stress, fertility, and more.

  • Registered acupuncturist with CAAA or equivalent
  • Experience in acupuncture and cupping therapy
  • Knowledge of TCM diagnostics and herbal medicine an asset
  • Empathetic and thorough in patient assessment
Apply Now

Osteopath

Full-time Calgary, AB

Help our patients move and feel better. We're looking for an experienced osteopath who brings both clinical expertise and genuine care to every session.

  • Diploma or degree in osteopathic manual practice
  • Member in good standing with OAO or equivalent
  • Experience treating musculoskeletal and systemic conditions
  • Collaborative mindset and excellent patient rapport
