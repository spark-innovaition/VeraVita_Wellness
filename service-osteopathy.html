Manual Therapy

Osteopathic
Manual Therapy

Hands-on osteopathic manual therapy to improve mobility, relieve musculoskeletal pain, and restore the body's structural balance and function through gentle, precise techniques.

Book a Session Book a Session

What to Expect

  • Full postural assessment and movement analysis
  • Hands-on assessment of joints, fascia, and soft tissue
  • Gentle osteopathic manipulative techniques
  • Craniosacral therapy where appropriate
  • Home exercise and postural guidance

Who It's For

  • Individuals with back pain, neck pain, or joint restrictions
  • Those recovering from injuries, surgery, or postural strain
  • Anyone seeking a non-pharmaceutical approach to musculoskeletal pain

Why VeraVita

Osteopathy treats the whole body as an interconnected system — not just the site of pain. Our practitioners address the structural compensations and fascial restrictions that drive recurring discomfort, creating lasting relief rather than temporary fixes.

Session Details

A thorough hands-on osteopathic assessment and treatment session addressing the structural root causes of your pain and restricted mobility.

Session Overview

  • Duration 60 min
  • Session Type 1-on-1 private
  • Includes Postural assessment, hands-on treatment, home exercises
