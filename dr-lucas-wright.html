About Dr. Hassan

Dr. Maya Hassan completed his Bachelor of Science in Biology at Queen's University and his Doctor of Naturopathic Medicine at CCNM, subsequently undertaking advanced training in reproductive naturopathic medicine. He works with both women and men navigating fertility challenges, recognising that optimal reproductive health depends on a whole-body approach for both partners.

At VeraVita, Dr. Hassan leads the clinic's fertility program, coordinating care with reproductive endocrinologists and fertility specialists to offer integrated support alongside conventional treatments such as IVF and IUI. His work extends beyond fertility to include comprehensive hormonal assessments and thyroid management — conditions that often underpin both fertility challenges and broader health concerns.

Dr. Hassan is known for his calm, reassuring manner and his ability to translate complex hormonal data into a clear, actionable plan. He believes that every patient deserves to understand their own biology and be empowered in their healthcare decisions.

What Dr. Hassan Can Help With:

  • Unexplained infertility in men and women
  • Preconception health optimisation for both partners
  • IVF and IUI preparation and support
  • Thyroid disorders (hypothyroidism, Hashimoto's, hyperthyroidism)
  • Male factor infertility and sperm health optimisation
  • Recurrent pregnancy loss
  • Testosterone imbalance and men's hormonal health

His Approach:

  • Comprehensive reproductive hormone panel and semen analysis for both partners
  • Targeted nutritional and supplemental protocols to optimise egg and sperm quality
  • Thyroid function assessment with optimal (not just "normal") reference ranges
  • Integration with fertility clinics — shared records and collaborative planning
  • Emotional and psychological support throughout the fertility journey
