What Is NAD⁺?

Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide (NAD⁺) is a coenzyme found in every living cell. It is essential for converting nutrients into cellular energy (ATP), repairing DNA, regulating circadian rhythms, and activating sirtuins — proteins that govern longevity and cellular stress response. NAD⁺ levels naturally decline with age, stress, poor diet, and chronic illness.

Intravenous NAD⁺ bypasses the digestive system entirely, delivering therapeutic doses directly to tissues at concentrations that oral supplements cannot achieve. This makes it one of the most powerful interventions available in functional and integrative medicine.

Key Benefits

  • Significant improvement in energy, stamina, and mental clarity
  • Support for mitochondrial function and cellular repair
  • Activation of longevity-associated sirtuin pathways
  • Neurological support — improved focus, memory, and mood stability
  • Accelerated recovery from chronic fatigue syndrome and long COVID
  • Support for addiction recovery and withdrawal symptom reduction
  • Enhanced athletic performance and post-exertion recovery

What to Expect

  • An initial consultation with Dr. Nazar to assess suitability and determine dosing protocol
  • Infusion sessions ranging from 2–4 hours depending on dose (typically 250–500 mg per session)
  • A course of 3–10 sessions recommended for therapeutic effect, with maintenance options available
  • Mild flushing, chest tightness, or nausea are possible during infusion — these are temporary and managed by adjusting drip rate
  • Most clients report noticeable improvements in energy and cognition within 24–48 hours of their first infusion

Who It's For

  • Individuals experiencing chronic fatigue, brain fog, or burnout
  • Those recovering from long COVID, post-viral illness, or chronic disease
  • Anyone pursuing healthy aging and longevity optimisation
  • High-performers seeking cognitive and physical peak state
  • Patients in addiction recovery programs (adjunct therapy)
