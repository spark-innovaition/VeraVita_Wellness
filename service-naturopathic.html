Your Naturopathic Doctor

Meet Dr. Jill Nazar, ND

Dr. Jill Nazar is VeraVita's Founder and Lead Naturopathic Physician. She takes a comprehensive, root-cause approach to health — combining the best of conventional diagnostics with evidence-informed natural therapies to address each patient's unique biology and goals.

Whether you're struggling with chronic fatigue, hormonal imbalance, fertility concerns, weight challenges, or supporting your child's health — Dr. Nazar builds a personalised care plan that evolves with you.

Areas of Care

What We Treat

A personalised naturopathic approach to common and complex health concerns — grounded in evidence and centred around you.

Fatigue & Energy

Chronic fatigue, low energy, and burnout addressed through nutrient repletion, hormone balancing, and targeted lifestyle interventions to restore your vitality.

Fertility & Hormones

Preconception support, menstrual irregularities, PCOS, thyroid imbalance, and perimenopause — natural hormonal restoration for every stage of life.

Weight Management

Sustainable, medically-guided weight loss programs addressing underlying metabolic drivers — not just calorie restriction — for lasting results.

Pediatric Health

Gentle, evidence-based naturopathic care for children — from immune support and digestive concerns to developmental wellness and allergies.

Chronic Illness

Comprehensive management of chronic conditions including autoimmune disease, inflammatory conditions, and complex multi-system presentations.

Digestive Health

IBS, bloating, food sensitivities, and gut dysbiosis treated with functional approaches — restoring your digestive foundation for whole-body health.
