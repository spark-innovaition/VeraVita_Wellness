What to Expect

  • Comprehensive fatigue intake covering sleep, stress, diet, and symptoms
  • Targeted lab testing including cortisol, thyroid panel, iron, B12, and vitamin D
  • Adrenal and thyroid support protocols
  • Personalised sleep optimisation plan
  • IV nutrient therapy recommendations if appropriate

Who It's For

  • People experiencing chronic exhaustion that doesn't improve with rest
  • Patients with suspected HPA axis dysregulation, hypothyroidism, or burnout
  • Anyone recovering from illness, surgery, or long COVID

Why VeraVita

Fatigue is one of the most prevalent and complex presentations we see. Our practitioners dig deeper — examining the hormonal, nutritional, and lifestyle patterns that are frequently overlooked in standard clinical assessments.

Service Details

Targeted hormone and nutrient testing to uncover the true root of your fatigue and rebuild lasting energy.
