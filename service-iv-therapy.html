Injectables & IV

IV Nutrient
Therapy

Customised intravenous nutrient infusions — from Myers' Cocktail to high-dose Vitamin C — delivering essential vitamins and minerals directly into the bloodstream for maximum absorption and effect.

Book a Session Book a Session

What to Expect

  • Pre-infusion health screening and bloodwork review
  • Custom IV formula selected based on your health goals
  • 45–60 minute comfortable infusion in a relaxing clinical setting
  • Oral supplement recommendations to extend results
  • Ongoing protocol for chronic conditions or performance optimisation

Who It's For

  • Individuals with chronic fatigue, immune dysfunction, or nutrient depletion
  • Athletes seeking athletic recovery and performance optimisation
  • Those recovering from illness or wanting preventive wellness support

Why VeraVita

Oral supplements are limited by digestive absorption. IV therapy bypasses this entirely — delivering nutrients directly into the bloodstream, bypassing the digestive system for rapid, reliable uptake. Our formulas are custom-designed based on your lab work and health goals, not one-size-fits-all bags.

Session Details

A fully customised IV infusion formulated to match your health goals, delivered in a comfortable clinic environment by a licensed practitioner.

Session Overview

  • Duration 45–60 min infusion
  • Session Type Clinic-based
  • Includes Pre-screening, custom IV formula, post-care guidance
