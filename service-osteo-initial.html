What to Expect

Your initial appointment is designed to build a thorough understanding of your health and create a foundation for effective treatment. Amy will take the time to listen to your concerns and develop a personalised approach to care.

  • Detailed review of your health history and current concerns
  • Discussion of your goals and what you hope to achieve
  • Thorough osteopathic assessment of your body's structure and movement
  • Initial hands-on treatment addressing findings from the assessment
  • Explanation of findings and a plan for ongoing care

Who It's For

  • New patients seeking osteopathic care for the first time
  • Individuals with musculoskeletal pain, joint restrictions, or chronic injuries
  • Those experiencing headaches, migraines, or digestive issues
  • Anyone looking for a whole-body approach to improving mobility and well-being

Why Osteopathy

Osteopathy is rooted in the understanding that the body functions as a connected whole. When movement is restricted or balance is disrupted, it can affect how the body functions over time. Rather than focusing only on symptom relief, treatment aims to address underlying patterns in a way that supports long-term function and resilience.
