What to Expect

  • Health intake and clinical postural assessment
  • Customised pressure and technique selection tailored to your needs
  • Targeted work on specific areas of restriction or pain
  • Guided post-treatment stretching for longevity of results
  • Personalised homecare recommendations for maintenance

Who It's For

  • People with muscle tension, chronic pain, or restricted mobility
  • Athletes or active individuals seeking recovery support
  • Anyone seeking regular evidence-based bodywork for wellness

Why Standard Therapeutic Massage

Therapeutic massage at VeraVita is delivered by a Registered Massage Therapist (RMT) — meaning your treatment is clinical, evidence-based, and designed to support structural health while remaining covered by most health benefits.
