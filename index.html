What Is Naturopathic Medicine?

Naturopathic medicine is a form of primary healthcare that treats the whole person — physical, hormonal, and emotional — rather than isolated symptoms. Naturopathic doctors use in-depth health assessments, advanced functional lab testing, nutrition, botanical medicine, and IV nutrient therapy to identify what's actually driving fatigue, hormonal imbalance, digestive issues, and weight concerns.

At VeraVita, our Naturopathic Medicine program is led by Dr. Jill Nazar, ND, and designed around your unique biochemistry and lifestyle.
