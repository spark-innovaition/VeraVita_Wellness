What Is Acupuncture?

Acupuncture is a Traditional Chinese Medicine technique that uses fine, sterile needles inserted at specific points on the body to relieve pain, reduce stress, and restore energetic balance.

Backed by centuries of clinical use and growing modern research, Traditional Acupuncture is commonly used for pain management, hormonal balance, fertility support, digestive health, and nervous system regulation.

Acupuncture in Calgary:
What to Expect

Your first acupuncture appointment at VeraVita begins with a discussion of your health history and treatment goals, followed by a personalized needling protocol. Most patients describe the sensation as a mild pressure or tingling rather than pain. Sessions typically run 60 minutes and may be combined with Tui Na Massage, Cupping, or moxibustion for enhanced results.

What Conditions Does
Acupuncture Treat?

Patients commonly seek acupuncture in Calgary for chronic pain, headaches and migraines, stress and anxiety, insomnia, digestive concerns, and hormonal or fertility-related issues. Acupuncture is also widely used to support the nervous system and complement conventional medical treatment, including Naturopathic Care.
