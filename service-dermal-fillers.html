Aesthetic Medicine

Dermal
Fillers

Hyaluronic acid-based dermal fillers expertly placed to restore lost volume, define facial contours, and achieve natural-looking rejuvenation with minimal downtime.

Book a Consultation Book a Consultation

What to Expect

  • Full facial consultation and volume assessment
  • Discussion of treatment areas and expected anatomical outcomes
  • Topical clinical numbing for comfort before injection
  • Precise placement using cannula or needle technique
  • Immediate visible results; full settling within 2–4 weeks

Who It's For

  • Adults experiencing volume loss in cheeks, lips, or under-eye areas
  • Those wanting to soften nasolabial folds or marionette lines
  • Patients seeking non-surgical, physician-led facial contouring

Why Dermal Fillers

Filler placement is an art form as much as a medical procedure. Our injector prioritises structural integrity and facial harmony — using only premium hyaluronic acid products and conservative amounts to avoid an overfilled appearance.
