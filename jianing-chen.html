About Jianing Chen

I am a dual-licensed practitioner specializing in acupuncture and evidence-based manual therapy. My clinical approach is highly customized to each patient's individual needs, whether you are seeking relief from acute muscle tension, support for recovery, or comprehensive relaxation.

My massage sessions are designed to target specific areas of tension, pain, or restriction. Depending on your goals, a treatment may incorporate a personalized combination of techniques, including deep tissue, relaxation, hot stone therapy, cupping, lymphatic drainage, sports massage, prenatal techniques, and assisted stretching.

What Jianing Can Help With:

  • Pain & Tension: Relieving muscle tightness, discomfort, and structural restrictions
  • Pregnancy Support: Pregnancy-safe positioning to relieve low back pain, hip/pelvic discomfort, and leg cramps
  • Postpartum Recovery: Relieving tension in the neck, shoulders, and hips while promoting relaxation after childbirth
  • Facial Therapy: Specialized facial massage to restore balance and reduce tension
