About Jianing Chen

I am a results-driven natural health practitioner with nearly two decades of experience helping patients overcome acute and chronic health challenges in Alberta. By holding licenses as an R.Ac, TCMP, and RMT, I am uniquely positioned to offer highly integrated treatment plans that draw upon the very best of Acupuncture, Tuina (Chinese Medical Massage), Moxibustion, and Cupping.

A significant portion of my clinical practice is dedicated to women's health and gynecological care. I have proven expertise in performing advanced breast lymphatic drainage techniques to relieve congestion and address lymphatic nodules, alongside regulating common gynecological issues such as dysmenorrhea and irregular menstruation.

I also have a deep specialization in pediatric and adolescent wellness. I regularly work with younger patients to successfully resolve challenges related to poor appetite, sleep deficiency, slow growth, and low weight conditions.

What Jianing Can Help With:

  • Pain Management: Chronic head, neck, shoulder, back, limb pain, and complex pain syndromes
  • Women's Health: Dysmenorrhea, irregular menstruation, and hormonal balance
  • Lymphatic Health: Advanced breast lymphatic drainage and reduction of lymphatic nodules
  • Pediatric Care: Growth retardation, low weight, poor appetite, and sleep deprivation in children and teens
  • Postural Wellness: Professional lifestyle, posture, and therapeutic exercise counseling

Her Approach:

  • Delivering comprehensive, tailored treatment plans that blend Acupuncture, Tuina, and Massage Therapy
  • Focusing on preventative medicine and traditional wellness techniques
  • Incorporating Moxibustion and Cupping therapy to enhance circulatory and structural healing
  • Providing active living coaching, utilizing martial arts expertise to teach Tai Chi for stress relief and core balance
