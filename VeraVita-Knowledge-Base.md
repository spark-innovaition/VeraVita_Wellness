### Insurance Coverage Notes

- Many extended health benefit plans in Alberta cover naturopathic medicine.
- Patients should verify coverage with their insurer **before** the appointment.
- Itemised/official receipts are provided for all services to support reimbursement claims.
- **Aesthetic services (Botox, dermal fillers) are typically not covered.**

### Payment Methods

- Visa, Mastercard, Debit, Cash, Direct billing (select providers).
- Payment is collected at the time of appointment.
- IV therapy and aesthetic treatments may require a **deposit at booking**.

### Referrals

- **No referral required** to see a naturopathic doctor or any practitioner at VeraVita.
- Patients may bring existing lab results, medications list, or notes from other providers as helpful context.

### Cancellation & No-Show Policy (terms-of-service.html)

- Minimum **24 hours' notice** required to cancel or reschedule.
- Cancellations under 24 hours may incur a fee.
- Repeated no-shows may require prepayment for future appointments.
- Late arrivals may result in a shortened session; full fee may still apply.

### First-Visit Expectations

- Initial naturopathic consultation: **60–90 minutes**.
- Practitioner reviews complete health history, lifestyle, diet, current medications, recent lab work.
- A personalised treatment plan is built.
- Patients should bring: list of current medications & supplements, recent lab results, provincial health card.

### Telemedicine / Virtual Consultations

- **Yes — offered** for follow-up appointments, supplement reviews, and select initial consultations where no physical exam is required.
- Conducted via secure, HIPAA- and PIPEDA-compliant patient portal.
- Patients should contact the clinic to confirm eligibility for their specific concern.

### Walk-Ins

- Operates by appointment. Same-day appointments occasionally accommodated based on availability — call ahead. Walk-ins not guaranteed.

### Privacy

- Adheres to Alberta's Health Information Act (HIA) and PIPEDA. Records stored in encrypted EHR; no third-party sharing without written consent (except as required by law).
