Therapy

Registered massage therapy targeting muscle tension, chronic pain, and stress relief — tailored to your needs through deep tissue, relaxation, and therapeutic techniques.

What to Expect

  • Initial intake discussing areas of concern and treatment goals
  • Skilled RMT assessment of muscle tension and postural patterns
  • Customised session blending deep tissue, Swedish, and trigger point work
  • Hydrotherapy and hot stone add-ons available
  • Stretch and self-care recommendations to extend results

Who It's For

  • Individuals with chronic muscle tension, neck pain, or lower back pain
  • Those seeking stress relief and nervous system relaxation
  • Athletes needing sports massage, injury recovery, or performance maintenance

Why VeraVita

Our registered massage therapists bring both technical skill and intuitive care to every session. Rather than a cookie-cutter experience, each treatment is adapted in real time based on what your body needs — deep pressure where needed, lighter touch where sensitivity demands it.
