What Is Osteopathy?

Osteopathy is a hands-on manual therapy that treats the body as a connected system, focusing on structural alignment, mobility, and the relationship between the musculoskeletal, nervous, and fascial systems.

Rather than isolating one problem area, an osteopathic therapist looks at how mechanical restrictions in one part of the body may be driving pain or dysfunction elsewhere, then uses manual techniques — sometimes alongside Therapeutic Massage — to restore balance and support the body's natural capacity to heal.

Osteopathy in Calgary:
Our Approach

At VeraVita, osteopathic care begins with a free 15-minute phone consultation to determine whether osteopathy is the right fit for your goals. From there, your Initial Appointment includes a full health history review, a thorough osteopathic assessment, and hands-on treatment to begin addressing mechanical restrictions, misalignment, or chronic tension patterns.

What Conditions Does
Osteopathy Treat?

Osteopathic manual therapy is commonly used for musculoskeletal pain, postural imbalances, mobility restrictions, joint stiffness, and nervous system regulation. Many patients also seek pediatric osteopathy to support a child's structural development. It pairs especially well with Massage Therapy for patients recovering from injury or chronic tension.
