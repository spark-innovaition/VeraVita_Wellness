Osteopathic Manual Therapy

A comprehensive hands-on approach using soft tissue manipulation, joint mobilisation, and myofascial release to restore structural balance and alleviate pain throughout the body. Effective for back pain, headaches, joint dysfunction, and post-injury recovery.

Structural Integration

A systematic program of deep fascial work that reorganises the body's connective tissue matrix — improving posture, reducing chronic tension patterns, and restoring ease and efficiency of movement from head to foot.

Alignment & Mobility

Targeted osteopathic techniques to restore optimal joint alignment and range of motion — addressing restrictions in the spine, pelvis, hips, shoulders, and extremities to improve functional movement and reduce compensatory patterns.

Paediatric Osteopathy

Gentle, highly specific osteopathic treatment adapted for infants, children, and adolescents — addressing birth-related strain patterns, colic, plagiocephaly, developmental concerns, and growing pains with exceptionally light-touch techniques.
